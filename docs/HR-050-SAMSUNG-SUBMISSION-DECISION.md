## Primary-source evidence and uncertainty

Samsung's [Consumer IP Control worksheet](https://image-us.samsung.com/SamsungUS/samsungbusiness/tv-ci-resources/Samsung-IP-Control.pdf) describes partner-created local-control drivers, TV approval and standby/wake settings. It shows intended third-party interoperability, but does not grant Hafa an endpoint-specific license or document every message Hafa uses. Hafa independently implements local device information, TV-approved secure WebSocket control, allowlisted commands/text and TV-returned app requests; it does not include Smart View SDK or use SmartThings cloud.

The [Samsung Developer Portal terms](https://developer.samsung.com/terms?location=us), effective January 29, 2026, address portal material and brand use. Their exact applicability to this independent local endpoint remains unresolved; they are not treated as automatic permission.

[Apple's review guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) require accurate, complete software/metadata and suitable hardware-review information. Rule 5.2.2 addresses permission under applicable third-party service terms and authorization on request. It is not a blanket manufacturer-letter rule. Original artwork and descriptive compatibility wording remain required. Sources were checked October 9, 2026 (Guam); no external outreach was sent.
