Hafa Remote controls compatible Samsung, Sony, and Vizio TVs over the local network. No account or test credentials are required.

Live test path: connect the review iPhone by Wi-Fi and a compatible TV by Wi-Fi or Ethernet to the same non-guest local network. Open the app and choose Add TV. The app searches automatically using the declared `_samsungmsf._tcp`, `_androidtvremote2._tcp`, and `_viziocast._tcp` Bonjour services; it does not scan the subnet. Tap a result and complete the brand-specific prompt on the TV: allow Hafa Remote on Samsung, enter the six-character code shown by Sony, or enter the four-digit PIN shown by Vizio. Manual address entry is available only under TV not showing up? as a troubleshooting fallback.

Samsung control uses its secure local WebSocket service on port 8002. Sony uses the local Android TV Remote v2 TLS pairing/control services on ports 6467 and 6466. Vizio uses its local HTTPS control service on the discovered allowlisted port (7345 or 9000). Trust is tied to the selected TV and pairing credentials remain in Keychain. Non-secret saved-TV metadata remains on device in SwiftData. Arbitrary internet loads are disabled. The app has no backend, analytics, ads, tracking, or in-app purchases.

Power off requires confirmation and preserves the saved TV. Samsung power-on is offered only after Hafa Remote has verified a Wi-Fi hardware address, then uses three unicast Wake-on-LAN packets to the last saved private address. For Sony and Vizio, power-on reconnects to the saved endpoint and uses the respective local standby command. Power On With Mobile, Remote Start/network standby, or Quick Start must be enabled as applicable; support varies by TV, firmware, and network. The UI does not report the TV online until reconnection succeeds. This build sends no UDP broadcast and requests no multicast entitlement. Text input is capability-dependent and may be ignored by individual TV apps or secure screens.

This build is for internal TestFlight household testing. It will not be submitted for external TestFlight or public App Review until the documented authorization, hardware-coverage, reliability, and reviewer-demo gates are complete.
