Hafa Remote controls compatible Samsung TVs over the local network. No account or test credentials are required.

To exercise the live flow, connect the review iPhone and a compatible Samsung TV to the same Wi-Fi network. Open the app and choose Add Samsung TV. The app discovers verified Samsung TVs through the local `_samsungmsf._tcp` Bonjour service; tap the TV, then choose Allow on the TV when prompted. The app will then open the remote. Manual address entry is available only under TV not showing up? for troubleshooting.

The app uses the TV's secure WebSocket control channel on local port 8002 and pins the certificate after physical approval. Discovery is Bonjour-based and does not scan the subnet or require the multicast entitlement. The initial capability lookup uses local HTTP only; arbitrary internet loads are disabled. Pairing credentials remain in Keychain. The app has no backend, analytics, ads, tracking, or in-app purchases.

Power off requires confirmation. When a saved Wi-Fi TV reports a valid hardware address, the offline power control sends three standard Wake-on-LAN packets by UDP unicast to the last verified local address, then attempts the secure connection. The UI says a wake signal was sent and does not treat the TV as on until it reconnects. Power On With Mobile must be enabled on the TV; success varies by TV and network. This build does not send UDP broadcast or include Apple's multicast entitlement. Text input requires a text field to be focused on the TV and may be ignored by individual TV apps or secure screens.

Review path: use a compatible Samsung TV with the live flow above. This build is intended for internal TestFlight and will not be submitted for public App Review until a demonstration video or in-build offline demonstration is available for reviewers without compatible hardware.
