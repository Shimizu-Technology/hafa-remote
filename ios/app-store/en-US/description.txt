Hafa Remote is a straightforward iPhone remote for compatible Samsung smart TVs on the same Wi-Fi network.

Open Add Samsung TV and Hafa Remote finds nearby compatible TVs automatically—no network address to type.

Everyday controls are close at hand:
• D-pad and select
• Volume and mute
• Home and back
• Play, pause, rewind, and fast-forward
• Power off with confirmation
• Native iPhone text entry on supported TV screens

Hafa Remote has no account, advertising, tracking, backend, or subscription. Commands stay between your iPhone and the TV on your local network.

Compatibility and limitations:
• A compatible Samsung TV with secure local-network control is required.
• Your iPhone and TV must be on the same non-guest Wi-Fi network.
• Initial setup requires approving Hafa Remote on the TV.
• Some apps and secure fields do not accept remote text.
