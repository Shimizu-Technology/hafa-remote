Hafa Remote is a straightforward iPhone remote for compatible Samsung, Sony, and Vizio smart TVs on the same local network.

Open Add TV and Hafa Remote finds nearby compatible TVs automatically—no network address to type. Pair once, save each TV, and switch between rooms from the remote.

Everyday controls are close at hand:
• D-pad and select
• Volume and mute
• Home and back
• Play, pause, rewind, and fast-forward
• Power off with confirmation
• Power on for saved TVs when the TV and network support the brand-specific local power path
• Native iPhone text entry on supported TV screens

Hafa Remote has no account, advertising, tracking, backend, or subscription. Commands stay between your iPhone and the TV on your local network.

Compatibility and limitations:
• A compatible Samsung, Sony, or Vizio TV with local-network control is required.
• Your iPhone must use Wi-Fi and the TV may use Wi-Fi or Ethernet on the same non-guest local network.
• Initial setup requires approving Hafa Remote or entering the code shown on the TV.
• Power on requires the TV's network standby, Remote Start, Quick Start, or mobile power setting, depending on brand and model.
• Some apps and secure fields do not accept remote text.
