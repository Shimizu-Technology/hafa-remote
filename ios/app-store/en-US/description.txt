Hafa Remote brings everyday TV controls to your iPhone with a clean native interface and no account, advertising, tracking, backend, or subscription.

It is being tested internally with compatible Samsung, Sony, and Vizio TVs. Compatibility depends on the exact model, firmware, and local-control service; support for every model is not promised.

Use D-pad/select, volume/mute, Home/Back, and playback controls. Optional Swipe navigation sends discrete movements, with Buttons always available as the accessible fallback. The Warm teal interface supports Light/Dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Increased Contrast, and Reduce Motion.

More Controls & Favorites offers features available for the selected TV:
• Samsung's source menu, available channel/guide/digit controls, and shortcuts from the TV's returned app list.
• Sony's negotiated configured app links and remote keyboard, when the TV service and focused field support them.
• Vizio's returned input choices and locally saved ordinary current-app favorites. Vizio guide, digits, and text entry are unavailable.

Save multiple TVs, name them, assign optional rooms, and switch through My TVs. Favorites and keyboard preferences belong to their TV. Recovery offers bounded reconnect attempts plus available wake, Retry Connection, and Find TV. A sent request does not confirm what happened on the TV.

Explore first: Help → Try the Remote Offline opens a clearly labeled simulation without contacting a TV. Help → Diagnostics can optionally record recent connection, delivery, and lifecycle events in memory. Preview a report before choosing whether and where to share it; nothing is uploaded automatically.

Live control requires your iPhone on Wi-Fi and a compatible TV on Wi-Fi or Ethernet on the same non-guest local network. Add TV searches for available TVs, with manual private-address setup as a fallback. First pairing requires the TV to be on and may require approval or its displayed code. Power-off preserves the saved TV. Power-on depends on the TV's network standby, Remote Start, Quick Start, or mobile-power settings and is not guaranteed. Individual TV apps or secure fields may reject text.

Commands and device records stay on the phone and local network during ordinary control. Pairing credentials remain in Keychain. You can choose to share a support report through the system share sheet; the chosen recipient then receives that report.
