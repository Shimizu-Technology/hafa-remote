Build 6 Control Center, accessibility, and resilient-connection regression:

1. Control Center: open My TVs, choose Add to Control Center, and follow the three in-app steps. Add the Hafa Remote control, leave the app, then tap the control. It should open the remembered remote without asking for a new account, permission, or pairing.
2. Alternate system placements: if useful, assign the same control to the Lock Screen or Action Button and confirm it opens the remote. The control is a launcher by design; it does not send a TV command while Hafa Remote is suspended.
3. Appearance and accessibility: check the empty state, setup, My TVs, Help & About, and the remote in both Light and Dark appearances. Repeat with Increased Contrast, Reduce Motion, VoiceOver, and the largest Dynamic Type size. Controls and recovery instructions should remain readable and reachable.
4. Capability truthfulness: confirm Samsung shows Keyboard while Sony and Vizio do not. Unsupported controls must not be shown merely because a TV belongs to a particular brand.
5. Foreground idle: connect to each Samsung, Sony, and Vizio TV, leave Hafa Remote open and untouched for at least five minutes, and confirm there is no spontaneous offline, Finding TV, or reconnect UI. Then send D-pad and volume commands; they should work immediately without a manual reconnect.
6. Intermittent control: keep each TV connected for at least ten minutes while sending occasional D-pad and volume commands. The remote should remain usable without periodic disconnect/reconnect cycles.
7. Background return: lock the iPhone or use another app for at least two minutes, return to Hafa Remote, and send a command. iOS suspends local-network connections in the background, so a brief automatic reconnect is expected; no button or re-pairing should be required.
8. Network interruption: briefly disable and restore Wi-Fi while connected. A momentary path flap should not immediately disable the remote. For a sustained loss, leave Hafa Remote open and confirm it keeps retrying until the saved TV becomes reachable, then resumes control without re-pairing.
9. My TVs persistence: save all household TVs with names and optional rooms, force-quit and relaunch Hafa Remote, and confirm every TV remains visible.
10. Saved-TV switching: switch among the Samsung, Sony, and Vizio at least ten times without using Add TV. Each selection should connect to the intended TV.
11. Address recovery: after a router restart or DHCP address change, select the saved TV. Hafa Remote should find it by device identity and update its cached address instead of requiring it to be added again.
12. Power regression: confirm Samsung, Sony, and Vizio power behavior still matches the TV settings and capabilities. Power-on requires the TV's network standby or mobile-power setting.

All tests require the iPhone and TV on the same non-guest local network. Pair each TV once while it is on; first-time pairing cannot be completed while a TV is off. No account or backend is used.
