Next internal candidate: household remote, recovery, accessibility, and support checks

Compatibility depends on the exact TV, firmware, and local-control service. Live control requires the iPhone and TV on the same non-guest local network. Pair while the TV is on. No account is required.

1. First launch: Help → Try the Remote Offline should work before adding a TV. It stays clearly simulated, without discovery, pairing, network commands, or changes to saved TVs. Try ordinary controls, power, playback, volume, and text.
2. Appearance: check first launch, Add TV, My TVs, Help, and the remote in Light/Dark, Increased Contrast, Reduce Motion, VoiceOver, and the largest Dynamic Type size. Inspect the Warm teal icon on the Home Screen and in Settings. Controls and recovery actions should stay reachable.
3. Pairing: Add TV, allow Local Network access, select the intended TV, and complete its approval/code. If discovery is unavailable, use TV not showing up? and select the correct brand. An address locates a TV; it must not replace saved identity or authorize another TV.
4. Saved TVs: rename/set rooms in My TVs, relaunch, then switch TVs at least ten times. Commands and favorites must follow the selection. A delayed command, launch, or wake started for A must not act on B.
5. Identity recovery: test changed TV addresses and repeated discovery rows. Known aliases should reconnect the same saved TV; ambiguous aliases require explicit selection. For rejected saved pairing, deliberately Forget TV in My TVs and add that TV again. Leave unrelated TVs alone.
6. Daily control: D-pad/select, volume/mute, Home/Back, and playback. Switch Buttons → Swipe → Buttons. One completed swipe produces one movement; Buttons remain the accessible fallback. Check the TV itself: a sent request does not confirm execution.
7. More Controls & Favorites: Samsung offers its source menu, available channel/guide/digit controls, and a TV-returned app list. Saving a favorite must not launch it; favorites stay with their TV.
8. Vizio: refresh/select returned inputs; save, name, and launch an ordinary current TV app. Casting/stream state is not saved. Guide, number pad, and keyboard remain absent.
9. Sony: app links require negotiation and are configured shortcuts, not installed inventory. When Keyboard is offered, focus a TV text field, send Unicode, disable/re-enable the per-TV preference, and check the useful missing/stale-focus message. Individual TV apps may reject text; Vizio has no text path.
10. Recovery: five minutes idle, ten minutes intermittent use, a two-minute background return, and Wi-Fi interruption. Foreground recovery makes up to five automatic attempts, then offers available wake, Retry Connection, or Find TV. It must not promise endless retry or silently re-pair.
11. Power: power-off preserves the saved TV. Test wake only where offered, with the TV's standby/mobile-power setting enabled. Unknown, asleep, reconnecting, and connected states must stay truthful. Wake is not guaranteed.
12. Diagnostics: Help → Diagnostics starts off. Enable recording, reproduce a problem, then Preview Support Report. Expect app/iOS versions, optional model/firmware, semantic events, and coarse timing; no names, addresses, identities, credentials, Wi-Fi name, or entered text. Share This Report opens the system share sheet for that exact preview; cancel without sending. Clear Events/disable clears live events. An open preview stays unchanged until dismissed and recreated.
13. Control Center: My TVs → Add to Control Center explains setup. Add the control and confirm it opens the remembered remote. It is a launcher, not a background TV command. Check Lock Screen/Action Button placement if used.

Report model, firmware, iOS/app version, the step, and what happened. Do not send addresses, codes/tokens, certificates, or entered text. Diagnostics are never uploaded automatically; share a preview only when you choose to.
