Hafa Remote is an iPhone-only remote for compatible smart TVs on the same local network. No app account or sign-in is required, and the app uses no manufacturer cloud service or SDK.

HARDWARE DEMO VIDEO: A video recorded on a physical iPhone with the TV in frame shows first launch, the Local Network permission, discovery, approving Hafa Remote on the TV, the remote controls, text entry, leaving and returning to the app, and power. The link is in the App Review Information notes.

WITHOUT TV HARDWARE: On the first-launch screen, tap Try Without a TV (also under Help > Try the Remote Offline). The offline demo is clearly labeled as simulated and makes no discovery, pairing or TV connections.

WITH TV HARDWARE: Put the iPhone on Wi-Fi and the TV on the same private, non-guest network (Wi-Fi or Ethernet). Turn the TV on, tap Add TV, allow Local Network access and select the TV. Choose Allow on the TV's approval prompt. If discovery cannot find the TV, use "TV not showing up?" to enter its address. Then try navigation, volume/mute, Home/Back and playback.

Optional sources, channel/guide/number controls, text entry and TV-returned app shortcuts vary by TV and current screen. Power-on depends on the TV's standby and network settings and is not guaranteed; the app distinguishes a wake attempt from a confirmed reconnection.

Diagnostics are off by default and kept in memory. Sharing requires previewing a redacted report and choosing a destination in the system share sheet. There is no automatic upload, tracking, advertising, subscription or backend.

Hafa Remote is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any TV manufacturer.
