Your everyday TV remote, right on your iPhone.

Hafa Remote brings a calm, simple interface to compatible smart TVs on your home network. No account, advertising, tracking, or subscription.

EVERYDAY CONTROLS
Navigate menus, use Select, Home and Back, adjust volume and mute, and control playback. Choose button or swipe navigation for comfortable one-handed use.

MORE WHEN YOUR TV SUPPORTS IT
Open the source menu, use channel, guide and number controls, type into supported text fields, and save shortcuts from the app list your TV returns. Available controls vary by model, firmware and TV app.

YOUR TVs, TOGETHER
Save multiple TVs in My TVs, give them names and optional rooms, and switch between them. Clear connection states and recovery actions help you retry or find a TV again.

SIMPLE LOCAL SETUP
Connect your iPhone by Wi-Fi and your TV by Wi-Fi or Ethernet to the same non-guest home network. Turn the TV on for first pairing, then choose Allow when your TV asks to approve Hafa Remote. A manual-address fallback is available if discovery cannot find it.

TRY THE INTERFACE OFFLINE
A clearly labeled simulated remote lets you explore the controls before pairing, without contacting a TV.

SHARING IS YOUR CHOICE
Pairing credentials and saved TV information stay on your phone during ordinary use. Optional diagnostics are off by default and keep a small, redacted history in memory. Preview a support report before choosing whether and where to share it. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

COMPATIBILITY
Hafa Remote works with select TVs that offer a local-network remote connection approved on the TV screen. Not every TV supports this, and features depend on the model, firmware, network and TV app. Power-on depends on the TV's standby and network settings and is not guaranteed.
